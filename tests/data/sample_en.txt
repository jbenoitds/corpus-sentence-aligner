Sleep and Wellbeing Study
Newsletter – Spring 2022
Dear participants,
Thank you for taking part in our study on sleep and wellbeing. Since January, 214 people have joined the project. This is more than we had hoped for!
What have we learned so far? Most participants sleep between 6 and 7 hours per night. About 40% report waking up at least twice during the night. Dr. Martin, who leads the study, says “these results are consistent with earlier research.”
Why does this matter? Poor sleep affects mood, memory and concentration. It can also make chronic pain feel worse.
Next steps
In the coming weeks, you will receive a short questionnaire by e-mail. It should take about 15 minutes to complete. Please answer every question, even if some seem similar. Your answers remain confidential.
Tips for better sleep
Go to bed and get up at the same time every day.
Avoid screens for at least one hour before bedtime.
Keep your bedroom cool, dark and quiet.
If you cannot fall asleep after 20 minutes, get up and do something relaxing (e.g. reading).
